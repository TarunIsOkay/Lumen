# Code of Conduct — Contributor Covenant 2.1

We pledge to make participation in Lumen a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, religion, or experience.

## Standards

Be respectful, constructive, and assume good intent. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, or unwanted sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Report issues to `hello@lumen.app`. All complaints are reviewed and handled confidentially.
